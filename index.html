How to play

놀이 방법

Exact match.
Close. Labels share a parent company; nationalities overlap; numbers are within a year or two.
No match. Arrows point toward the answer.

Idol scores your guess against eight facts, Group against six. Face slides a photocard out of its sleeve one band at a time. Song gives you 1 second, then 2, 4, 7, 11 and 16.

Daily is the same puzzle for everyone and resets at midnight your time; it leans toward groups you already know. Endless draws from everyone in the game, deep cuts included.
